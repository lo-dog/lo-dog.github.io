Event Posters

Collaborating with the Dubversified collective in Victoria BC, I've crafted a collection of event posters that blend visual design with electronic music culture. Each poster represents a unique event, combining typography, illustration, and digital techniques to capture the energy and atmosphere of the underground music scene. These designs showcase my passion for creating visuals that resonate with both artists and audiences.

TCG pendants

An ongoing project of mine is turning trading card game cards into wearable pendants. Pokemon, Yu-Gi-Oh, Magic: The Gathering, and other TCG cards all make it into the mix. I collect primarily Japanese cards and put them in a hard plastic sleeve. Following the art style and color of the card I use thrifted fabrics to decorate the card and finally string it up to be worn. I was heavily influenced by Mcowls a good friend of mine who takes mcdonald fry packages and uses spray paint to turn them into small art pieces that people wear at festivals. It has been a lot of fun finding fabric at thrift shops that fit the colorways of the cards perfectly, each pendant is a small visual puzzle I have to solve!
