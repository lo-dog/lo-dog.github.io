Overview

Circular Supply is a Canadian company that helps industrial and healthcare teams recycle PPE instead of sending it to landfill. I built their public marketing site and a production web portal that handles pickups, credits, invoicing, and ESG reporting for both admin staff and client organizations.

Both projects are live:

  • circularsupply.org — marketing website
  • portal.circularsupply.org — authenticated web portal

Web Portal

The portal is a full-stack application for managing PPE recycling operations. It supports role-based access for Circular Supply admins and client organizations, with separate views and permissions for each.

Admin Portal
  • Dashboard — operations overview, PPE weight metrics, active sites
  • Client management — accounts, contacts, program details
  • Site management — client locations with Google Maps integration
  • Pickup management — record pickups with automatic credit calculations
  • Scheduling — calendar view for pickup requests and recurring schedules
  • Circular Credits — track earnings, redemptions, and tier progress across clients
  • Invoices — billing with Xero API integration and PDF export
  • Notifications & email — templates, history, and automated alerts
Client Portal
  • Dashboard — environmental impact overview and credit balance
  • Pickups — history, status tracking, and CSV export
  • Scheduling — request and view upcoming pickups
  • Circular Credits — earned credits, redemptions, and reward tiers
  • Invoices — view and download billing documents
  • Notifications — in-app alerts for pickup and credit updates

Credits are earned at 3 Circular Credits per kg of accepted PPE, expire after 12 months (FIFO), and can be redeemed through a tiered rewards system.
